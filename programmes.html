Academic Programmes

From the flagship MBA to a four-year digital science degree and a research-driven Ph.D. — each programme blends rigorous fundamentals with live industry engagement.

MBA

Master of Business Administration

IIM Mumbai's flagship MBA gives a strong foundation across finance, marketing, operations, HR, and analytics, blending classroom theory with real industry exposure through corporate ties, guest lectures, and projects. Faculty comprising esteemed academicians and industry stalwarts, and collaborations with prestigious international institutions like MIT and Kellogg School of Management round out a global-caliber learning experience.

2 yr
Full-time
MBA–OSCM

Master of Business Administration - Operations and Supply Chain Management

Builds on IIM Mumbai's deep, decades-long reputation in operations and supply chain (rooted in its NITIE legacy), with a curriculum enriched by global certification programs on supply chain digitization and analytics. The program is consistently ranked among the top business schools in India for this domain, emphasizing experiential learning and industry collaboration.

2 yr
Full-time
MBA–SM

Master of Business Administration -Sustainability Management

A trailblazer in South Asia since 2001, this program blends managerial and technical perspectives to weave ESG, environmental management, safety, and CSR into business strategy. Students cover GHG accounting, sustainable finance, circular economy design, and ESG law/policy, preparing them to lead organizations toward the UN SDGs.

2 yr
Full-time
BS–DSBM

Bachelor of Science - Digital Science and Business Management

A pioneering four-year, full-time residential undergraduate programme combining digital technologies (AI, cloud, IoT, quantum computing) with core business fundamentals. It features dedicated labs (Quantitative Modelling, Business Analytics, Decision Science, etc.), industry internships every year, and an optional integrated BS+MBA fifth-year pathway for top performers.

4 yr
Residential
PGP–Ex

Post Graduate Programme for Executives

A one-year post graduate program for executives that provides a unique blend of advanced management insight and a robust technical foundation, covering operations, OBHR, marketing, finance, data science, and sustainability, with a specialization option in the third module and full IIM Mumbai alumni status on completion.

1 yr
Executive
PGDEx–VLFM

Visionary Leadership in Manufacturing (VLFM)

A one-year full-time residential executive diploma jointly conducted by IIM Mumbai and IIT Delhi to build techno-managerial leaders for India's manufacturing renaissance, featuring 1,300+ contact hours, an 8-week industry internship, and international treks, aligned with "Make in India."

1 yr
Executive
PhD

Doctoral Programme in Management (PhD)

A full-time doctoral program building rigorous, evidence-based research capability with exposure to advanced analytical tools such as Generative AI, Agentic AI, and ML. Specializations span Operations & Supply Chain, Analytics & Data Science, Finance/Economics/Strategy, Marketing, Sustainability, and OB/HRM, with stipend support and on-campus accommodation for scholars.

4–5 yr
Full-time
Exec PhD

Executive Doctoral Programme in Management

Designed for professionally employed persons pursuing the doctoral programme while continuing their duties of service, without institute financial assistance; research is largely carried out at the scholar's own organization under faculty guidance, open to senior government/PSU officers or managers from large, stock-listed private firms.

3–4 yr
Executive
