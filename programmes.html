Grading System & Credit Equivalency

Courses at IIM Mumbai follow a rigorous grading matrix with 1 credit typically corresponding to 30 contact hours. All instruction and examinations are conducted strictly in English.

1 Course (3 Credits) ~30 Class Hours = 6 ECTS / 3 US Credits
Typical Term Workload 4 to 5 Courses (12 to 15 IIM Credits / 24–30 ECTS)
Language of Instruction 100% English medium across all lectures & materials
