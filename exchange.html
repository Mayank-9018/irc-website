Student Exchange Programme

To provide cross-cultural exposure and cultivate a global perspective, IIM Mumbai is actively expanding its International Student Exchange Network. Through strategic partnerships with leading business schools worldwide, the program enables students to immerse themselves in diverse academic environments, navigate international business dynamics, and build lasting global networks.

INBOUND MOBILITY

Studying at IIM Mumbai as an Exchange Student

IIM Mumbai welcomes graduate management students and undergraduate scholars from partner institutions worldwide. Below are the comprehensive academic guidelines, nomination procedures, document requirements, and evaluation policies.

ACADEMIC FRAMEWORK

Academic Requirements & Workload

Academic Requirements

To do a term at IIMM, one should be currently an MBA student or equivalent in a post programme or BS/BBA student or equivalent in undergraduate programme. He/She must have completed the foundation course at the home institution.

Workload

Each of our courses has 30 contact hours corresponding to 3 credits.

Medium of Instruction

All our classes are conducted in English.

Nomination Process

Exchange Coordinator of host university should send us formal nomination through link provided with student’s details such as student first name, last name, valid email id and current degree etc.

We will send a letter of acceptance, visa request letter and information package once we hear from the exchange coordinator.

📌 A brief orientation programme will be held before the term begins.

Required Documents

  • ✓ Completed application form
  • ✓ Passport-size photograph
  • ✓ Copy of Passport pages showing date of birth, nationality and address
  • ✓ Copy of valid Visa
  • ✓ Official transcripts of all completed courses in the current program
  • ✓ Proof of Health Insurance with International Coverage
COURSE SELECTION

Electives Offered

IIMM electives cover a wide array of topics like Economics, Finance, Marketing, Supply Chain and Operations Management. Students follow a bidding system to select the elective courses of their interest.

EVALUATION SYSTEM

Grading System

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Exchange Student Guidelines & FAQs

What are the living expenses in Mumbai?

On-campus hostel accommodation is highly subsidized and guaranteed for international students. Average living expenses for food, local transport, and utilities typically range between USD 250–350 per month.

Do I need to speak Hindi to study in Mumbai?

No. 100% of the academic coursework, case discussions, library materials, and campus services operate in English. Mumbai is an international cosmopolitan city with widespread English fluency.

How do I obtain an Indian Student Visa?

Once officially nominated by your home university and accepted, IIM Mumbai's International Relations Office will issue an official Admission & Visa Support Letter to facilitate your Student Visa application.

What facilities are available on campus?

The 63-acre campus includes high-speed Wi-Fi, air-conditioned smart classrooms, a 24/7 library, sports courts (badminton, tennis, basketball), medical center, gym, cafeterias, and walking trails around Powai Lake.
