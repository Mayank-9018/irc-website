ABOUT THE CITY

The Gateway to Modern India

Originally an archipelago of seven islands inhabited by indigenous Koli fishermen communities, Mumbai has transformed into one of the world’s top ten centers of commerce by global financial flow.

Home to the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE), the National Stock Exchange (NSE), and the headquarters of Fortune 500 multinationals and Indian conglomerates (Tata, Reliance, Aditya Birla), Mumbai generates over 6% of India’s GDP and accounts for 70% of maritime trade and capital transactions.

Beyond its business dominance, Mumbai is world-renowned for its artistic energy, being the home of Bollywood (the world's largest film producing industry), dynamic theater circuits, seaside Art Deco promenades, and a cosmopolitan culture welcoming scholars, entrepreneurs, and artists from across the globe.

EXPLORE & DISCOVER

Must-Visit Places in Mumbai

From iconic seaside monuments and UNESCO heritage architecture to tranquil national parks and buzzing art districts, here are the essential destinations every exchange student should experience.

Colaba • Historic Landmark

Gateway of India & The Taj Mahal Palace

Built in 1924 overlooking the Arabian Sea, this 26-meter Indo-Saracenic basalt arch is Mumbai’s defining symbol. Adjacent stands the grand Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, opened in 1903.

📍 South Mumbai (Colaba) 🚢 Ferry port to Elephanta
Seaside Promenade

Marine Drive (The Queen’s Necklace)

A sweeping 3.6-kilometer C-shaped coastal boulevard lined with palm trees and the world's second-largest collection of Art Deco buildings. Spectacular at sunset and illuminated at night.

📍 South Mumbai 🌅 Best at Sunset & Evening
UNESCO World Heritage

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus

An architectural masterpiece combining Victorian High Gothic revival with traditional Indian palace architecture. Opened in 1887 with gargoyles, stained glass, and majestic stone domes.

📍 Fort District 🚆 Central Railway Hub
Engineering Marvel

Bandra-Worli Sea Link

An 8-lane cable-stayed bridge spanning the open waters of Mahim Bay, connecting the Western Suburbs to South Mumbai with breathtaking panoramic ocean and skyline views.

📍 Bandra to Worli 🚗 Iconic Coastal Drive
UNESCO World Heritage

Elephanta Caves

Ancient 5th–7th century rock-cut cave temples on Elephanta Island in Mumbai Harbour, famous for the monumental 20-foot three-headed sculpture of Trimurti Sadashiva.

📍 Elephanta Island ⛵ 50-min ferry from Gateway
Culture & Art Quarter

Kala Ghoda Art Precinct

Mumbai’s premier heritage art district housing the Jehangir Art Gallery, National Gallery of Modern Art, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Vastu Sangrahalaya (Museum), artisan cafes, and indie bookstores.

📍 Fort District 🎨 Galleries & Boutiques
Nature & History

Sanjay Gandhi National Park & Kanheri

One of the world's largest urban national parks located just north of Powai, featuring 109 rock-cut Buddhist caves dating from the 1st century BCE, lush trekking trails, and leopard reserves.

📍 Borivali (Near Powai) 🌿 Forest Treks & Cycling
Lifestyle & Bollywood Hub

Bandra: Queen of the Suburbs

Known for seaside promenades at Bandstand and Carter Road, historic Portuguese chapels (Mount Mary Basilica), vibrant street graffiti in Ranwar Village, and trendy artisanal bakeries.

📍 Western Suburbs ☕ Cafes, Nightlife & Walks
IIM Mumbai Neighborhood

Powai Lake & Hiranandani Gardens

Surrounding the IIM Mumbai campus, Powai features a picturesque lake, neoclassical high-rises, thriving startup headquarters, lakeside jogging paths, international restaurants, and cafes.

📍 At IIM Mumbai's Doorstep 🛍️ Dining, Lakes & Startups
STUDENT LIFE EXPERIENCES

What to Do in Mumbai

Make the most of your international exchange by immersing yourself in the authentic flavors, culture, and rhythm of Mumbai.

01

Embark on a Culinary & Street Food Trail

Taste Mumbai's quintessential snacks: Vada Pav (the spiced potato burger), Pav Bhaji at Juhu Beach, Bhelpuri & Sev Puri at Chowpatty, and coastal Malvani seafood curries. Don't miss cutting chai and Irani bun maska at century-old cafes like Kyani & Co.

Vada Pav Irani Cafes Coastal Seafood Filter Coffee
02

Explore Art Deco & Victorian Heritage Walks

Take an early morning architecture walking tour from Fort to Oval Maidan. Marvel at the dramatic contrast between Victorian Gothic courts and libraries on one side and glamorous 1930s Art Deco apartment blocks on the other.

Oval Maidan Art Deco Tour Flora Fountain Horniman Circle
03

Experience Mumbai’s Transit & Dabbawalas

Witness the world-famous Mumbai Dabbawalas in action at Churchgate Station—a Six Sigma-certified logistical marvel delivering 200,000 home-cooked tiffins daily with near-zero errors using coded wooden crates.

Dabbawala Network Local Trains Chalo App Mumbai Metro
04

Shop at Iconic Bazaars & Modern Malls

Browse antique curios, vintage jewelry, and handicrafts at Colaba Causeway and Crawford Market, or experience world-class luxury shopping, multiplexes, and international designer brands at Phoenix Palladium and Jio World Drive.

Colaba Causeway Crawford Market Jio World Plaza Phoenix Palladium
05

Celebrate Vibrant Cultural Festivals

If you're studying during the Autumn or Winter terms, witness the magnificent 10-day Ganesh Chaturthi procession, the brilliant lights and sweets of Diwali, and the electric Garba dances during Navratri.

Ganesh Utsav Diwali Lights Dandiya Raas Kala Ghoda Fest
06

Plan Weekend Getaways into the Western Ghats

Mumbai's location makes weekend trips effortless. Catch a scenic train to the mist-covered hills of Lonavala & Khandala, take a speed-boat to the beaches of Alibaug, or tour India's wine country in Nashik Valley.

Lonavala Hills Alibaug Beaches Nashik Vineyards Matheran Eco-Station
PRACTICAL EXCHANGE GUIDE

Navigating Mumbai: Tips for International Students

Practical advice to make your stay in Mumbai comfortable, safe, and exciting.

🚖 Getting Around

Auto-rickshaws operate on strict electronic meters in the suburbs (including Powai). App-based cabs (Uber, Ola) are ubiquitous and affordable. For buses and metro, download the Chalo App for contactless ticketing.

🌤️ Weather & Climate

Mumbai enjoys warm, tropical weather year-round. Monsoons (June to September) bring refreshing rains; Winter (November to February) offers pleasant, balmy evenings with temperatures around 18°C–28°C.

🗣️ Language & Communication

English is universally spoken across universities, restaurants, shopping centers, and transit. Hindi and Marathi are the primary local languages. People in Mumbai are known throughout India for being warm, helpful, and welcoming.

🛡️ Safety & Medical Care

Mumbai is widely regarded as one of India's safest cities, with active street life well past midnight. IIM Mumbai provides a 24/7 on-campus dispensary and ambulance, with premier multi-specialty hospitals located within 15 minutes in Powai.
