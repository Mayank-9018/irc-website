ABOUT IIM MUMBAI

From NITIE to IIM Mumbai —
Building Leaders for a Global Future

The Indian Institute of Management Mumbai (IIM Mumbai), formerly NITIE Mumbai, was established in 1963 by the Government of India with the assistance of the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) and the International Labour Organization (ILO).

Today, IIM Mumbai is a leading management institute committed to developing future-ready leaders through rigorous, industry-oriented education, analytical thinking, innovation, and ethical leadership.

Located in Mumbai, India’s financial capital, the institute benefits from a vibrant business ecosystem and strong industry connect. Its academic strengths span Operations & Supply Chain, Analytics, Finance, Marketing, HR, Information Technology, Project Management and Sustainability.

With a strong emphasis on experiential learning, research, and global perspectives, IIM Mumbai prepares students and professionals to navigate complex business environments and contribute meaningfully to organisations and society.
