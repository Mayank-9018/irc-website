COOPERATION MODES

Avenues of Academic & Research Cooperation

01

Bilateral Student Mobility

Reciprocal term exchanges with 100% tuition waivers for graduate (MBA) and undergraduate students, supported by campus accommodation and academic credit transfers.

02

Faculty & Scholar Exchanges

Visiting professor fellowships, co-teaching of advanced electives, joint case study development, and reciprocal sabbatical opportunities.

03

Joint Research & PhD Fellowships

Collaborative research in Operations Research, Supply Chain Resilience, ESG Finance, and AI for Business, including joint doctoral thesis supervision.

04

Custom Immersions & Study Tours

Bespoke 2–3 week India Immersion programs for international cohorts featuring Mumbai corporate visits, cultural workshops, and startup ecosystem engagement.
