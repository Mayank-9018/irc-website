INFRASTRUCTURE & AMENITIES

Campus Facilities

Sports & Fitness Centre

IIM Mumbai campus houses several sports and fitness facilities including cricket, football ground, tennis court, open gym, basketball court, badminton courts, and a volleyball court. In addition, the campus also has numerous indoor sports and games facilities such as Table Tennis, Billiards, and Carom.

Fitness Centre (Gym) facility is also available on campus.

Health Centre

Student well-being is a top priority.

Our on-campus dispensary operates 24/7, supported by a dedicated emergency ambulance service available around the clock.

Hostel Block

The campus features three modern hostel blocks—including one of the region's tallest residential towers and a dedicated Management Development Program (MDP) building for executive stays.

Pond

Walking and jogging trails surround a peaceful pond nestled right at the heart of the campus.

Banking & ATMs

The campus features an on-site bank branch and two ATMs that accept all major international debit and credit cards.

Library

Our fully equipped library offers extensive access to leading academic journals, databases, subscriptions, and case studies.

Hours of Operation:
  • Monday – Friday: 9:00 AM – 12:00 AM (Midnight)
  • Saturday & Sunday: 11:00 AM – 12:00 AM (Midnight)

Internet Facilities

  • Students are required to bring their own laptops.
  • Upon enrollment, each student receives an official university email account.
  • The campus is fully equipped with high-speed Wi-Fi, alongside dedicated LAN connectivity in all hostel rooms.
DINING SERVICES

Food

Students can have their meals in the mess or at various eateries in the campus on a direct payment basis. Mess is managed by the student community, and provides four meals per day in both Indian vegetarian and non-vegetarian cuisine.

HOUSING & RESIDENCES

Accommodation

  • On-Campus Residence: All Exchange students will be provided on-campus accommodation.
  • Living Charges: The accommodation charges for 2026-27 are Rupees 45,000 per month for sharing accommodation at student's hostel. These charges are per student and should be paid before coming to IIM Mumbai within beginning & before 5 days of each month.
  • Campus Guidelines: The exchange students are expected to adhere to the general rules applicable for staying on campus.
